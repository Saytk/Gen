## 1. Shared rules (every spell)

### 1.1 The feed timeline

For a feedable spell (RMB, Space, E), with `N` = flames fed (0–3, never more than the flames in the orbit at the press),
`I` = feed interval (0.3 s; **0.15 s while ablaze**, Combustion) and `C` = the spell's `CastTime`:

> **anticipation(N) = N · I + C** (the feed phase is the held part; the cast phase starts on release, or
> automatically when the 3rd flame lands or the orbit is empty).

| Spell | `C` | N = 0 | N = 1 | N = 2 | N = 3 | N = 3 ablaze | After the anticipation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RMB Great Fireball | 0.5 s | 0.5 | 0.8 | 1.1 | **1.4** | 0.95 | Projectile 25 → 22 → 19 → 16 m/s |
| Space Meteor Leap (take-off) | 0.1 s | 0.1 | 0.4 | 0.7 | **1.0** | 0.55 | Flight `LeapDuration` 0.45 s [GEN, Plan 2] |
| E Flame Pillar | 0.4 s | 0.4 | 0.7 | 1.0 | **1.3** | 0.85 | Telegraph 0.8 s, then impact (press → impact 1.2 / 1.5 / 1.8 / 2.1 s) |

The cast bar already draws this exactly (`GenCastBar::ComputeLayout`). The timing source is always the GA timer, never
an anim notify (Art Bible §8 "Gameplay sync", §8.4 #4). Animation and VFX are **authored to it** (§1.4).

**The threshold event.** A threshold is reached when the displayed fed count (`AGenCharacterBase::GetFedResource()`)
**increases**. It drives four things on every client, and nothing on the dedicated server:
1. the Hearth: one flame leaves its socket and flies into the spell (§5);
2. the hand charge (or the feet gather, for the leap) steps up one size (§1.3);
3. the caster's aim indicator snaps to its next size (§1.2);
4. a soft tick sound (`SC_Combat`), the audio half of the cue.

A **decrease** (release spends the flames, a cancel or interrupt returns them, a server correction of ±1) never pops:
it only updates sizes. On the owner the count is predicted (immediate); other clients get the server's estimate,
corrected by the client's report (`ServerReportFedResource`), so they see the pops about ½ RTT later. That is cosmetic
and matches the replicated feed montage, which is server-timed too.

### 1.2 Indicators

**Three kinds**, never mixed up:

| Kind | Who sees it | Driven by | Spells |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aim indicator** | The caster only (local, never replicated) | The active ability computes its own geometry from its own gameplay values each frame (footprint = hitbox by construction, Art Bible §7.1 rule 1) | RMB line + splash cap; E range arc (its circle is Plan 2's area preview); Space range arc + landing circle + ring stubs; LMB/Pyroblast line **only if the player turns on an option** (⚑ F11) |
| **Cast telegraph** (self-centred) | **Everyone**, in their relation colour | Replicated `FGenCastInfo` (owner: predicted) + the ability's own radius | F Combustion (during its 0.5 s cast); R Living Flame (during its 0.5 s form) |
| **Delayed-area telegraph** | Everyone | `AGenGroundArea` (Plan 2) | E Flame Pillar after release (0.8 s) |
| **Flight telegraph** (decision 2026-10-08) | Everyone, in their relation colour | The replicated landing point and the ability's `LandingRadius` (new, see Plan-Visuals "Open points") | Space Meteor Leap's landing circle during the flight (~0.45 s, ⚑ F5) |

**Rendering (all three).** `M_VFX_Telegraph`, unlit translucent, on flat plane meshes 2 cm above the floor (the
"unlit translucent projected mesh" option of Art Bible §7.5, as `AGenGroundArea` already does). No lit decals. Border
**2 px at 1080p** in the relation colour at α 0.90, constant on screen through `fwidth`, plus the 1 px keyline whose
polarity comes from `MPC_TeamColours.KeylinePolarity`. Fill α 0.20 for self and ally, α 0.15 with static hazard
stripes for an enemy (UI §2.10, §4.12). No widget or material hard-codes a colour: the relation colour comes from the
MPC through `RelationIndex` (1 self, 2 ally, 3 enemy, 4 neutral).

`M_VFX_Telegraph` gets one scalar, **`Shape`**, and two sizes in centimetres, **`SizeX` / `SizeY`** (half-extents of the
plane), so every border is computed in world units and stays 2 px wide whatever the plane's stretch. One master, one
uniform branch, no static switch (Art Bible §3.2: keep permutations low). This edits a hot shared file: **go-ahead
given by the user on 2026-10-08** (Plan-Visuals Task E1).

| `Shape` | Name | Geometry | Vocabulary (Art Bible §7.5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Disc | Circle of radius `SizeX`; timer fill grows from the centre (`Fill`) | Circle = get out (existing) |
| 1 | Lane | Rectangle `2·SizeX` long × `2·SizeY` wide, flat start, rounded end | Line = projectile (existing) |
| 2 | Range arc | Thin arc of radius `SizeX`, **±30° around the aim**, border only, α 0.45, no fill | New, caster-only: "how far" (⚑ F7) |
| 3 | Stub | Lane whose fill and border fade to 0 over the last 40 % | Line = projectile (a ring Fireball's first metres) |
| 4 | Spokes | 6 short radial ticks on a disc border, pointing outward | New: "knockback from here" (⚑ F8) |

**Layering.** `TranslucencySortPriority`: ally telegraphs 1 < own aim indicators 2 < enemy telegraphs 3 (Art Bible
§7.5: enemy marking sorted above ally decals; your own aim never hides an enemy telegraph's border) < projectile ground
markers 4 (small). Heroes are opaque and depth-tested, so no indicator ever draws over a body.

**Growth at thresholds: snap, never lerp.** At each threshold the size jumps to its new value in the same frame. A
lerp would show, for a few frames, a size that is neither the old nor the new hitbox, and Art Bible §7.5 forbids
overstatement. **No brightness pop on the indicator itself**: the size step is the cue, and a pop on a 3.5 m disc
would be a large-area flash (UI §5.4). The pop lives on the hand charge and the Hearth (small areas).

**When shown and hidden (aim indicators).** Shown from the press (feed start, or cast start for unfed spells) until
**the aim is locked** (end of the cast, when the target data is sent, `ReleaseCast`). Hidden at once on cancel,
interrupt, death, or when another spell replaces the cast. Never shown on the dedicated server or to other players.

### 1.3 VFX rules applied to Curffe

- **One saturated core colour per spell: fire.** Core `#FFF0C2`, body `#F5B82E`, edge/smoke `#7A5230` (Art Bible §4.3),
  **no saturated red**, no team hue on any core. Team identity lives only on the ground markers, outlines and telegraph
  borders.
- **Material: `M_VFX_Erode`** (Art Bible §7.4): painted stepped shape in R, panning noise in G, erosion threshold from a
  Niagara Dynamic Parameter **stepped at 12 fps per particle age** (`floor(Age*12)/12`, never on global `Time`), a hard
  `SmoothStep` edge, a rim band. **Colour bands from three vector parameters** (core/body/edge) instead of a Curve
  Atlas for v0 (⚑ F10). It is a hot shared master: **go-ahead given on 2026-10-08** (Task E1).
- **Three layers** (§7.1): primary = hitbox-defining core or border (crisp, highest value range); secondary = trails and
  wisps at 50–70 % opacity; tertiary = sparks and embers, brief, desaturated.
- **Niagara, shader-first.** Mesh + erode shader for rings, columns and shockwaves; small flipbooks only for smoke puffs;
  2-layer ribbons for trails (dark alpha back + bright additive front). Every system: CPU sim, **fixed bounds**,
  **pooling `Auto Release`**, one of four Effect Types (`ET_VFX_Filler`, `ET_VFX_Skillshot`, `ET_VFX_CC`, `ET_VFX_Ult`,
  Art Bible §7.8) with `bCullByGlobalBudget` off; secondary and tertiary particles scale 50 / 75 / 100 % on Low / Medium /
  High; **enemy CC and ultimate primaries and telegraphs are never culled**.
- **No new Niagara user parameters in v0.** Size follows the spawned component's scale (radius / reference radius), as
  Plan 2 already does for areas. Reason: VibeUE issue #464 (adding a user parameter through the engine toolset can
  hard-crash the editor and corrupt the system). If one is ever needed, it is added by hand in the Niagara editor with a
  non-empty default, then verified.
- **Dynamic lights.** ≤ 4 alive on High (oldest culled), ≤ 2 on Medium, 0 on Low. Curffe uses at most three kinds, all
  short and shadowless: Great Fireball impact at N ≥ 2, Flame Pillar impact, Combustion eruption. **No light on the
  Fireball stream** (it would hold the cap permanently).
- **Impacts.** Frame 1 = full-size flash at the **exact** hitbox radius, unstepped; 2–4 frames at peak; ≤ 50 % opacity
  and saturation within ~0.2 s; linger within the tier cap. Big areas flash their **border ring and a column, not the
  whole disc at full value** (⚑ F2: Flame Pillar's size exception accepted on 2026-10-08, ring first).
- **Per-viewer.** An ally's non-threatening effects should render at 50–70 % opacity and saturation on other clients
  (§7.2). Every clean route needs a per-instance input on the Niagara system (a user parameter, blocked by issue #464,
  or custom primitive data read by `M_VFX_Erode`, unverified for Niagara renderers in 5.8.3). **v0 ships ally effects at
  full intensity** and keeps the hook in C++ (the spawner knows the viewer relation). ⚑ F25. Telegraphs, markers and
  rings are per-viewer from day one (they use `RelationIndex`).
- **Status visuals** stay in the one tag-driven path of Plan 2 Task 9. This spec adds an optional attached Niagara
  system per state (`FGenStatusVisual::System`, `Socket`), spawned locally while the tag is present (tags replicate
  already; no extra RPC). Each state keeps a unique **shape** (§7.6).
- **Flash governor and photosensitivity.** Curffe's impact flashes count with everyone else's (Art Bible §7.2, §9.1);
  the per-threshold pops are small-area (hand and orbit) and at most 3 per spell, so they stay under 3 per second per
  box.

### 1.4 Animation mechanism

**Three phases, one montage each** (Art Bible §8.3 "charged ability = `ChargeMontage` + `CastMontage`", plus the
"feed loop" row; §8.4 #1 "no combo trees built on section jumps; use one montage per step"):

| Phase | Montage property | When it starts | Play rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Feed** (feedable spells only) | `FeedMontage` (**new**) | Feed start (`StartFeeding`), **also fixes Art Bible §12 Q41**: opponents now see the gathering pose for the whole feed | `L_step / I_active` | Three sections `Feed_1`, `Feed_2`, `Feed_3`, each authored **one interval long** (0.30 s = 9 frames at 30 fps) and linked in order; `Feed_3` links to itself (safety only: feeding ends at the 3rd threshold at the latest). At rate 1 the section boundaries fall **exactly on the thresholds** (the feed ticks are scheduled from the feed start too), so each flame meets a slightly stronger pose without any section jump. Ablaze: `I` = 0.15 s, rate 2 |
| **Charge** (anticipation) | `ChargeMontage` | Cast start (`StartCasting`), after the feed | `L_charge / CastTime` | The held anticipation pose; **the cast flash is only its first 0.1–0.25 s** (§7.3). Ends exactly at the release, so the anticipation never outlasts the gameplay window (§8.3, Yasuo rule) |
| **Cast** (release) | `CastMontage` | Release (`ReleaseCast`) | 1, or `L_cast / TargetDuration` | Action pose in **frames 0–2**, smear 1–2 frames, held follow-through ≤ 0.3–0.4 s. `TargetDuration` (virtual, 0 = rate 1) is set only where the release phase has a gameplay length: leap flight (`LeapDuration`), Backfire window (`CounterWindow`), Living Flame form (`FormDuration`) |
| **Land / Finish** | `LandMontage` (Plan 2) / `FinishMontage` (Living Flame, new) | Landing / form end | 1 | Follow-through only |

- **Play rate formula** (pure, tested): `rate = clamp(L / T, 0.5, 2.5)`; outside **[0.8, 1.25]** (except the ablaze feed at
  2.0) the editor logs a warning: the clip should be re-timed to the GA value (Art Bible §8.2, authoring step 4), because
  a heavily sped anticipation reads wrong. `L` is the authored section or montage length, read at runtime
  (`UAnimMontage::GetSectionLength`), never typed in.
- **Replication.** Every phase plays through `UAbilityTask_PlayMontageAndWait` so the ASC replicates it (§8.4 #1),
  with its rate (replicated in `RepAnimMontageInfo`). Each new phase replaces the previous one in the same slot group
  (blend-in 0.05 s, blend-out 0.1 s [GEN]). The owner predicts its own; proxies start each phase at position 0, which
  is fine because every phase is short.
- **Slots.** Hand spells and Backfire: `UpperBody` (the legs keep `BS_CastStrafe`, the caster moves slowly while
  casting). Leap and Living Flame burst: `DefaultSlot` (full body). Combustion: `UpperBody` with big shoulder motion (he
  can still move during its cast). Both slots exist in `ABP_Champion` (binary scan): **no `ABP_Champion` change**.
- **Root motion.** All Curffe clips are in place. Leap montages keep root motion enabled in the asset and play with
  `AnimRootMotionTranslationScale = 0` (`CastMontageRootMotionScale = 0`, Plan 2): the jump force moves the capsule.
  Never hit-stop a root-motion montage (§8.4 #6).
- **Notifies.** No gameplay notify anywhere (projectiles, areas and leaps are timed by the GA). Cosmetic notifies only:
  `AnimNotify_GameplayCue` on frame 0 of each Cast montage (`GameplayCue.Curffe.<Spell>.Release`: hand flash, smear,
  release sound), **`bTriggerOnDedicatedServer` unticked on every placement**, tick type Queued (§8.2). The §10 #16 notify
  audit runs on the new montages.
- **Hit-stop.** None on Curffe's spells in v0: every heavy hit he lands carries a knockback or a stun (§8: never freeze a
  CC or knockback victim), and his own montages are replicated (≤ 90 ms cap would apply).
- **Interrupt.** Unchanged: an interrupted feed or cast stops the playing phase through the task's cancel binding; the
  server's release failure stops the client's throw (`ClientStopCastMontage`, Plan 2 Task 5).

### 1.5 Channel display (Backfire window, Living Flame form)

UI §4.5 already defines a **channel** bar: the overhead cast row drains right to left, seen by everyone. Two Curffe
states are timed windows that opponents must read: Backfire's 1.2 s window ("wait it out") and Living Flame's 0.5 s form
(when the ring will hit). Both are shown as channels through `FGenCastInfo` (new `bChannel` flag), which also feeds the
self-centred cast telegraph of Living Flame. Combustion's 0.5 s is a normal cast (fills left to right).

## 3. Spells in detail

### 3.1 LMB Fireball (Filler, 2) and Pyroblast (during Combustion)

**Indicator.**
- **Default: none.** Art Bible §7.2 gives Filler "Telegraph: none" and §7.1 rule 8 reserves aim lines for aimed or
  charged skills. A permanent lane on a held, auto-repeating basic attack is floor clutter.
- **Option (off by default):** "Show basic attack aim line" in the gameplay settings (client-only, `UGameUserSettings`).
  When on: a Lane from the spawn point (`SpawnForwardOffset`) to the projectile's `MaxRange`, clipped at the first wall,
  width = 2 × collision radius × `ShotScale` (1 for Fireball). Self colour, fill 0.15 (lighter than skillshots).
  ⚑ F11.
- **Pyroblast** follows the same rule. With the option on, its lane shows its own width and a splash cap at its
  explosion radius (1.2 m).

**VFX beats.**

### 3.2 RMB Great Fireball (5 → 8 with flames)

**Indicator (caster only).**

| Part | N = 0 | N = 1 | N = 2 | N = 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lane length | `MaxRange` (13 m) from the spawn point, clipped at the first wall | same | same | same |
| Lane width | 2 r₀ | 2 r₀ · s(1) | 2 r₀ · s(2) | 2 r₀ · s(3) = 2 r₀ · `ScaleAtMaxFeed` |
| Splash cap at the lane end | — | — | Disc, radius `ExplosionRadius` (1.5 m) | same + **spokes** (knockback) |

`r₀` = the projectile class's collision radius (read from the CDO), `s(N) = GenFeeding::ScaleByFeed(1, ScaleAtMaxFeed, N,
MaxFeed)`, the exact scale the server applies (`SpawnProjectile`). The cap sits where the lane ends (range or wall),
because the projectile explodes at whatever it meets first; the cap shows the explosion size, not a promised location.
Thresholds come from the ability (`ExplosionMinFeed` = 2, `KnockbackMinFeed` = 3). Shown from the press to the aim
lock. Self colour, fill 0.20.

**Others see:** no ground decal (Art Bible §7.5 skillshots, §12 Q10). They read the spell from the feed pose (now
replicated, Q41 fix), the flames leaving the orbit, the **hand charge growing one step per threshold**, the overhead cast
bar with its ticks, and then the projectile size and speed.

## 5. The Hearth (custom state)

**Goal:** an opponent counts the flames in one glance at the gameplay camera, from any team's view, including 0
(Art Bible §7.6, §10; spec §8).

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Sockets | **Always 5 sockets** at fixed 72° spacing, orbiting together. **Lit socket = one flame.** Count = lit sockets. Today's code re-spaces the visible flames around the circle, so 3 flames look like a triangle and 2 like a line: the count is hard to read and the gaps don't show what is missing. Fixed sockets make "3 of 5" a pattern, and they generalise the Art Bible's "0 flames = five dim sockets" to every count |
| Orbit | Radius 70 cm, 30 cm above the capsule centre (chest height, under the overhead stack), **60°/s** [GEN] (today 120°/s: slower reads better when counting) |
| Lit flame | Camera-facing teardrop (a plane instance turned toward the local camera in C++ each frame; no WPO billboard, no static switch), **16 cm tall ≈ 19 px at 1080p** (≥ 6 px rule), pale core `#FFF0C2`, amber body `#F5B82E`, stepped 12 fps flicker, `MI_VFX_Erode_Hearth` with the painted teardrop texture `T_VFX_Teardrop` |
| Empty socket | Dim ember dot, 6 cm (≈ 7 px), `#7A5230` at low value. Always drawn, so "empty mage" (0 lit) reads as five dark dots |
| Feed (threshold) | The highest lit socket goes dim and its flame **flies to the spell's socket** (hand for RMB and E, feet for the leap) in **0.12 s** [GEN] along a short arc, then merges with the hand-charge pop |
| Cancel / interrupt | The fed flames fly back to their sockets (0.12 s), relighting them |
| Release | Fed flames are spent: they are already in the spell, nothing flies |
| Leap flight | **v1:** the fed flames orbit **tight** (25 cm) and fast around the body until they burst as the ring (needs the carried count, §3.3). v0: nothing extra |
| Gain +1 (Fireball hit), +1 (regen) | Next socket lights with a 2-frame pop (hit) or a 0.2 s fade-in (regen: quiet) |
| Gain +2 (Backfire block) | Two flames fly from the block point into the next two sockets |
| Refill (Living Flame) | The 5 sockets light one after another, 50 ms apart |
| Unlimited (Combustion) | Flames ×1.4, longer tails; a fed flame leaves and its socket relights at once (the refill after every spell) |
| Team colour | None (team identity stays on the outline and ring, §4.3). Flames vs energy: told apart by **shape and place** (teardrops orbiting the body vs a round orb on the floor or a segmented UI bar), §7.6 |
| Network | Driven by replicated `Resource` (attribute) and `FedResource` (owner: predicted), as today. Count = `floor(Resource) − Fed` |
| Performance | **One instanced static mesh component, 5 instances**, per-instance custom data (lit, pop, flight alpha): **1 draw call per Curffe**, no Niagara, no tick on the dedicated server |
